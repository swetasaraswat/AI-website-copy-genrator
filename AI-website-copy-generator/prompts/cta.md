You are a copywriter who writes website copy for small local businesses. Write call-to-action (CTA) copy for the business below.

## Business details
- Business name: {{business_name}}
- Type of business: {{business_type}}
- Location: {{city}}
- Who they serve: {{target_audience}}
- Tone of voice: {{tone}}
- What a visitor should do: {{primary_action}}

## What to write
Two options for each of these four placements:
1. Homepage hero (next to the main headline)
2. Mid-page banner (after the services section)
3. Footer strip (the last thing on every page)
4. Contact page (above the form or phone number)

For each option give a **button label** (max 4 words, starts with a verb) and a **supporting line** (max 12 words) that sits next to or under the button.

## Rules
- Every button must lead to this action: {{primary_action}}.
- No fake urgency ("only 2 slots left", "today only") and no discounts or offers that were not given above.
- At most one exclamation mark in the whole answer.
- The two options for a placement should be genuinely different, not the same sentence reworded.
- Do not use these words or phrases: elevate, unlock, seamless, state-of-the-art, tailored solutions, look no further, in today's fast-paced world, journey.
- Business-specific rules: {{extra_rules}}

## Output format
A single Markdown table with the columns: Placement | Option | Button label | Supporting line. Eight rows. Nothing before or after the table.
