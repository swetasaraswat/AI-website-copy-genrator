## Sections to write
1. **Page title** – for search results, max 60 characters, includes the service and the city.
2. **Meta description** – max 155 characters.
3. **Headline (H1)** – max 10 words.
4. **Intro** – 2 to 3 sentences: who this service is for and what it helps them with.
5. **What's included** – 4 to 5 bullet points.
6. **Who it's for** – 2 sentences.
7. **FAQ** – 3 questions people really ask, each with a 1 to 2 sentence answer.
8. **Closing call to action** – one line plus a button label (max 4 words).

## Rules
- Write the way a friendly local owner would talk. Short sentences, everyday words.
- Use only the facts given above. Do not invent prices, durations, discounts, awards, testimonials or statistics.
- The details of this service (what is included, how long it takes, what it costs) are usually NOT given. Where you need one, write [ADD: what is missing] so the owner can fill it in. Do not guess.
- Do not use these words or phrases: elevate, unlock, seamless, state-of-the-art, tailored solutions, look no further, in today's fast-paced world, journey.
- Business-specific rules: {{extra_rules}}

## Output format
Markdown. One heading per section, numbered exactly as above, with the copy underneath. No introduction and no closing remarks.
